Offline First-Aid & Field Survival Protocols

Medical / Heatstroke

Heat Exhaustion & Dehydration

  • Move victim immediately to shade or air-conditioned vehicle.
  • Loosen tight clothing and apply cool damp cloths to neck and forehead.
  • Sip ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts) or coconut water slowly; avoid caffeinated drinks.
  • If vomiting or confusion occurs, call 108 immediately.
Wildlife / Terrain

Snakebite & Insect Stings

  • Keep the patient calm and immobilize the affected limb below heart level.
  • DO NOT cut, suck poison, or tie tight tourniquets.
  • Remove rings, watches, and tight footwear before swelling starts.
  • Rush to nearest district government hospital for Anti-Snake Venom (ASV).
Safety & Harassment

Feeling Followed or Unsafe

  • Enter any well-lit hotel lobby, petrol pump, or government bank ATM immediately.
  • Dial 112 or 1091 loudly so nearby persons notice.
  • Activate YATRALENS siren to draw public attention and deter perpetrators.
  • Stay on a live speaker call with trusted contacts until police arrive.
Navigation

Getting Lost in Remote Areas

  • Stay in one open location rather than wandering deeper into unknown trails.
  • Use a bright flashlight or whistle (3 short blasts is the universal SOS signal).
  • Conserve phone battery: switch to Airplane mode between periodic GPS checks.
  • Look for highway milestone markers or river downstream paths to reach settlements.
